Good morning, colleagues. This newscast covers August nineteenth to September eighteenth, two thousand twenty-six. The month was dominated by the European Society of Cardiology Congress in Munich.

The new European heart failure guidelines keep only two phenotypes: reduced ejection fraction, below fifty percent, and preserved ejection fraction. Mineralocorticoid receptor antagonists become class one regardless of ejection fraction. Semaglutide or tirzepatide receive class two a in preserved ejection fraction with obesity. Critical reading: it simplifies things, but these drugs run into availability and cost issues in the Dominican Republic.

The Australian Staree trial enrolled nine thousand nine hundred seventy-one adults aged seventy or older without cardiovascular disease. With atorvastatin, major cardiovascular events were six point zero versus eight point three percent, with a hazard ratio of zero point seven zero. Critical reading: disability-free survival did not change, but it supports not withholding statins on the basis of age alone.

The Librexia trial, in fourteen thousand one hundred ninety-four patients with acute coronary syndrome, was neutral. With milvexian, cardiovascular death, myocardial infarction, or ischemic stroke occurred in five point four versus five point one percent with placebo. Critical reading: another setback for factor eleven inhibition; safe, but without efficacy.

The German Tric I H F trial compared transcatheter tricuspid repair with medical therapy in three hundred sixty patients. Survival free of death or heart failure hospitalization at one year was seventy-three point nine versus forty-eight point four percent. Critical reading: the trial is open-label and single-country, and the therapy is inaccessible on grounds of cost in the country.

In nine hundred eighty-six patients, performing transcatheter aortic valve implantation first was non-inferior to performing percutaneous coronary intervention first. The one-year composite was twenty-two point two versus twenty-four point two percent. Critical reading: it allows freedom of sequencing in simple anatomies, choosing valves with good coronary access.

In rural China, nearly thirty-four thousand hypertensive patients received intensive blood pressure control from non-physician community health workers. At seven years, dementia occurred in eight point eight five versus ten point five five percent. Critical reading: a scalable model transferable to the Caribbean, although dementia diagnosis in rural settings has limitations.

The Enrich A F trial gave edoxaban after intracranial hemorrhage to nine hundred forty-eight patients. It did not reduce stroke or embolism, and major bleeding was eleven point six versus five point two percent. Critical reading: the direct oral anticoagulant does not pay off in this group; left atrial appendage closure remains an alternative to study.

In transthyretin cardiac amyloidosis, eplontersen did not reduce cardiovascular death and recurrent events in one thousand four hundred thirty-two patients. The rate ratio was zero point eight nine. Critical reading: the widespread use of tafamidis or acoramidis likely diluted the effect.

In left-sided endocarditis, the Poet Two trial showed that twenty-six days of antibiotics were non-inferior to forty-one days. But relapses were five point one versus one point six percent. Critical reading: it saves two weeks but triples relapses, and it calls for close follow-up.

The Fifth Universal Definition of Myocardial Infarction sets three profiles: primary, secondary, and procedure-related, with identical criteria after coronary intervention or surgery. Critical reading: requiring a wall motion abnormality or loss of viability may underdiagnose, and the chairs have industry ties.

In South Korea, the Single A F trial anticoagulated one thousand eight hundred three patients with a single risk factor. The twenty-four-month composite was zero point five versus one point five percent. Critical reading: the first randomized data in this gray zone, but with few events, open-label, in an Asian population.

In India, nurse-coordinated care in one thousand five hundred seven heart failure patients lowered mortality by twenty-two percent. Critical reading: a cheap intervention, replicable in our setting, although open-label and single-country.

Aficamten became the first positive myosin inhibitor in non-obstructive hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, in five hundred seventeen patients. Critical reading: the effect is modest, Cytokinetics sponsored the trial, and serial echocardiography is needed.

In atrial fibrillation, in two hundred sixty-two patients under double-blind conditions, ablation did not outperform a sham procedure on quality of life. Critical reading: part of the symptomatic improvement is a placebo effect, although the trial is small and short.

Finally, Score Two for Latin America and the Caribbean was published, calibrated with mortality data from thirty-two countries. Critical reading: it is the first version for the region, but it is not based on Dominican cohorts. Thank you for listening. Until next month.
