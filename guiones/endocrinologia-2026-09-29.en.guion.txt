Good morning, colleagues. This summary covers August thirtieth to September twenty-ninth, twenty twenty-six. It was a heavy month for diabetes and obesity, and a thin one for thyroid and pituitary disease. We gathered fourteen verified items.

The United States Food and Drug Administration approved once-weekly insulin efsitora alfa, Onswik, for adults with type two diabetes only. Four open-label phase three trials with more than three thousand four hundred adults showed glycated hemoglobin non-inferior to glargine or degludec. Critical reading: control is equivalent, not superior, and the trials were open-label and manufacturer-funded. The gain is convenience and adherence.

Bayer announced that the agency extended finerenone to chronic kidney disease associated with type one diabetes. In the Fine One trial, with two hundred forty-two patients, albuminuria fell twenty-eight percent at month six. Hyperkalemia was ten point one versus three point three percent. Critical reading: the endpoint is a marker, not hard kidney or cardiovascular events, and the trial was small and Bayer-funded.

Diabetologia published the updated consensus of the American and European diabetes associations on type one diabetes in adults. It emphasizes new technologies and incorporates teplizumab. Critical reading: it is an expert consensus, not a guideline with formal evidence grading, and its technologies remain unevenly accessible in the Dominican Republic.

Beta Bionics announced clearance of its Mint insulin patch pump, with launch planned for the first quarter of twenty twenty-seven. Critical reading: no comparative clinical data have been published, and the clearance covers the device, not the closed-loop system.

Tirzepatide gained a cardiovascular indication in type two diabetes on August twenty-eighth, outside the period. The Surpass C V O T trial compared it with dulaglutide in thirteen thousand two hundred ninety-nine adults. The hazard ratio was zero point nine two, with an interval of zero point eight three to one point zero one. It met non-inferiority without showing superiority. Critical reading: the comparator had its own cardiovascular benefit, so the correct reading is not superior.

The European diabetes congress opened in Milan on September twenty-eighth. Lilly presents Triumph two, with retatrutide in obesity with type two diabetes: mean weight loss of up to twenty point eight percent at eighty weeks. Critical reading: almost all are company topline results without peer review, and none changes prescribing today.

In obesity, the Endocrine Society published a scientific statement setting six research priorities, including targets beyond weight and long-term safety. Critical reading: it is a roadmap for researchers with no new clinical recommendations, but timely where these drugs are prescribed for cosmetic reasons.

According to Chinese media, a Chinese dual agonist showed phase three topline weight loss of up to nineteen point three percent. A Chinese semaglutide biosimilar fell within the equivalence margin against the originator. Critical reading: these are company data in an exclusively Chinese population, not yet peer reviewed. The signal is industrial, since such biosimilars could lower costs in Latin America.

According to Perfil, Argentina's regulator approved semaglutide seven point two milligrams weekly for obesity. The Step Up trial showed mean weight loss of approximately twenty-one percent at seventy-two weeks. Critical reading: the information comes from the company, with no link to the official disposition.

In Brazil, according to Folhapress, at least twelve new pens were registered this year after the Ozempic patent expired. Generics must be at least thirty-five percent cheaper. Critical reading: it is a price reference for the Caribbean, but interchangeability data are lacking for most of these pens.

In thyroid disease, a Houston group studied eighty-one patients with medullary thyroid carcinoma. Osteoprotegerin was much higher in patients with bone metastases. Critical reading: it is a single institutional group, with no sensitivity figures or cutoffs, and the authors call for validation.

The agency approved zilurgisertib for fibrodysplasia ossificans progressiva from age twelve. The primary endpoint was three point one versus sixteen point seven percent, not significant. Approval rested on lower new heterotopic bone volume. Critical reading: a failed primary endpoint, a small cohort and an ultra-rare disease.

A narrative review describes a U-shaped relationship between testosterone and atrial fibrillation. It proposes a target of three hundred fifty to five hundred fifty nanograms per deciliter on replacement therapy. Critical reading: it is not a meta-analysis, and the range is inferred from observational data without any prospective trial.

The Central American and Caribbean Endocrinology Congress took place in Punta Cana in late August, outside the period. Critical reading: we found no published abstracts or results, so it is a completed agenda item, not evidence.
