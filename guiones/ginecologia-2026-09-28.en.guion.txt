We begin with the Lancet Seminar on preeclampsia. It consolidates a definition without mandatory proteinuria. An angiogenic factor ratio of thirty-eight or less rules it out within seven days. Its negative predictive value is ninety-nine point three percent. Nightly aspirin, one hundred fifty milligrams or less, started before week sixteen, reduces preterm preeclampsia by about sixty-two percent. Critical reading: it is a narrative review with no new data. But its algorithm applies in the Dominican setting, where hypertensive disorders remain the leading cause of maternal death.

Respiratory syncytial virus vaccination in pregnancy. A meta-analysis of twenty-four studies found seventy-nine point seven percent effectiveness against infant hospitalization at three months. In the trials, preterm birth was more frequent with the vaccine, with a relative risk of one point one seven. It was not significant after excluding the Grace trial. Critical reading: the safety signals are weak, but they reinforce the thirty-two to thirty-six week window and blood pressure monitoring.

In the Dominican Republic, the Ministry of Public Health surveillance bulletin reports ninety-eight maternal deaths through week thirty-five. That compares with one hundred twenty-five in the same period of two thousand twenty-five, twenty-one point six percent fewer. Critical reading: these are provisional figures without denominators, and underreporting is not addressed. Still, the trend is downward.

The European Medicines Agency's human medicines committee recommended approval of senaparib as first-line maintenance in advanced ovarian cancer. In the Flames trial, with four hundred four patients, median progression-free survival was not reached, versus thirteen point six months with placebo. The hazard ratio was zero point four three. Grade three or higher adverse events reached sixty-six point three percent. Critical reading: the trial was run only in China, there are no overall survival data, and severe hematologic toxicity is high.

A Spanish series of four hundred laparoscopic sacrocolpopexies, with a median follow-up of thirty months, showed prolapse recurrence of seven point eight percent. Among symptomatic women, de novo stress incontinence appeared in twenty-three percent. Critical reading: there is no comparator, but nearly one in four is a concrete figure for informed consent.

In reproduction, a retrospective cohort of six hundred twenty-nine donor oocyte recipients assessed preimplantation genetic testing for aneuploidy. It was not associated with fewer transfers to the first live birth, with a hazard ratio of zero point eight nine. Critical reading: it is retrospective and single-center, but consistent with biology. Aneuploidy is low in young oocytes, so it should not be offered by default in egg donation.

The Australia and New Zealand registry compared vitrified and fresh donor oocytes. The live birth rate per transfer favored fresh oocytes by less than two percentage points. By the journal podcast presenters' calculation, about fifty-three transfers with fresh oocytes would be needed for one additional live birth. Critical reading: it is a retrospective registry confounded by selection. Even so, it supports vitrified egg banks as a reasonable alternative.

The United States Food and Drug Administration accepted for review the extension of the Miudella copper intrauterine device from three to six years. At three years, the cumulative Pearl Index was one point zero five in women aged seventeen to thirty-five. Critical reading: the press release presents no data for years four to six, and the product is not yet available in the region.

In Nature Medicine, menopause was associated with a blood proteomic signature of sixteen proteins. In older women, the score was associated with incident Alzheimer-type dementia, with a hazard ratio of one point one five. Critical reading: the effect is modest, with the lower bound of the interval near one. It did not evaluate hormone therapy, so it should not be used to promote or discourage it.

In platinum-sensitive relapsed ovarian cancer, the single-arm L Moca study in China and Malaysia enrolled two hundred twenty-four patients on maintenance olaparib. Median overall survival was fifty-one point zero months. Critical reading: it confirms earlier figures in an Asian population, but without a comparator; its value is consistency, not novelty.

Finally, a Turkish cohort compared laparoscopic lateral suspension, pectopexy and sacrocolpopexy. Twelve-month apical success was seventy-four point one, seventy-three point zero and eighty point six percent, with no significant differences. Critical reading: it does not demonstrate equivalence and does not justify abandoning sacrocolpopexy as the reference standard.
