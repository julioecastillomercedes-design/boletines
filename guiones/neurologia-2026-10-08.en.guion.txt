Good morning, colleagues. This neurology bulletin covers September eighth to October eighth, twenty twenty-six. It includes eleven verified items. We found nothing verifiable and substantive on amyotrophic lateral sclerosis within the window.

We begin with stroke. The Lais trial, published in the Journal of the American Medical Association, tested the intravenous neuroprotectant loberamisal in nine hundred ninety-eight Chinese adults. At ninety days, a modified Rankin score of zero to one was reached by sixty-nine point seven percent. With placebo it was fifty-six point three percent. Mortality was one point two versus two point zero percent. Critical reading: the trial was funded by the manufacturer, without thrombectomy and in moderate stroke, and it needs replication outside China.

The United States Food and Drug Administration approved tavapadon for Parkinson disease on September twenty-eighth. It is a partial agonist at dopamine receptors one and five, taken once daily. In the Tempo three trial, on time without troublesome dyskinesia increased by one point seven hours, versus zero point six hours with placebo. Critical reading: it is a new mechanism, but no trial compared it with levodopa or with other dopamine agonists.

In multiple sclerosis, the agency accepted fenebrutinib under priority review. It is an oral inhibitor of Bruton tyrosine kinase. Versus teriflunomide, the annualized relapse rate fell by fifty-one point one and fifty-eight point five percent. In primary progressive disease it was noninferior to ocrelizumab, without superiority. Critical reading: liver enzyme elevations were more frequent, and an unspecified imbalance in deaths needs clarification.

A negative result came from the movement disorders congress in Seoul. In the Luma trial, with six hundred forty-eight patients with early Parkinson disease, an inhibitor of leucine-rich repeat kinase two did not slow progression. The hazard ratio was one point zero four. Critical reading: it remains unclear whether brain penetration was sufficient, or whether the target matters only in mutation carriers.

In epilepsy, Xenon submitted its application for azetukalner in drug-resistant focal seizures. In the Ex Tole two trial, median seizure reduction was fifty-three point two percent with twenty-five milligrams. With placebo it was ten point four percent. Dizziness occurred in twenty point five percent. Critical reading: tolerability at the higher dose is a concern, and head-to-head data against modern antiseizure medications are lacking.

Satralizumab received priority review for myelin oligodendrocyte glycoprotein antibody-associated disease. A decision is expected by January tenth, twenty twenty-seven. In the Meteoroid trial, it reduced the risk of first relapse by sixty-eight percent. Critical reading: this disease has no approved therapy, but full data have not yet been published.

In Alzheimer disease, a single-center cohort followed one hundred ninety-seven patients treated with lecanemab. Thirty-four plasma proteins changed with the number of infusions. Critical reading: it opens the door to blood markers for deciding when to stop the antibody, but these are unvalidated candidates.

In menstrual migraine, the Luna trial of atogepant reduced perimenstrual migraine days by one point two zero, versus zero point four zero with placebo. Critical reading: the difference is less than one day per cycle, and there is only a company press release so far.

In Brazil, the national health regulator approved oral atogepant for prevention of episodic and chronic migraine. Critical reading: price and public coverage will determine its real impact.

Vutiglabridin, a metabolic agent, improved motor scores by two point eight three points in early Parkinson disease at eight hundred milligrams. Critical reading: it is a small signal, from a single country, in a non-peer-reviewed abstract.

Finally, the agency cleared Micsi Pet, artificial intelligence software that quantifies neurological positron emission tomography. Critical reading: no performance metrics or independent validation have been published.
