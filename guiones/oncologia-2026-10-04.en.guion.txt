Good morning, colleagues. This is the oncology bulletin for September fourth to October fourth, two thousand twenty-six. The month was dominated by the World Conference on Lung Cancer, the International Myeloma Society meeting and the American radiation oncology congress. There were fifteen verified items; six come from China and Brazil.

We start with Harmoni two, a phase three trial with three hundred ninety-eight patients. It compared the bispecific ivonescimab with pembrolizumab as first-line therapy for advanced non-small cell lung cancer. All tumors expressed programmed death ligand one. Median overall survival was thirty point eight versus twenty-two point six months, with a hazard ratio of zero point seven three. Serious treatment-related adverse events occurred in twenty-nine point nine versus twenty-one point six percent. Critical reading: the trial was run only at centers in China, and confirmation in global studies is still lacking.

The eight-year update of Adaura included six hundred eighty-two patients with resected lung cancer and an epidermal growth factor receptor mutation. It compared three years of adjuvant osimertinib with placebo. Eight-year overall survival was seventy-nine versus sixty-four percent, with a hazard ratio of zero point five two. It reinforces that every resected tumor should be genotyped, which is still not routine in the Dominican Republic. The trial is sponsored by AstraZeneca.

Destiny Lung zero four compared trastuzumab deruxtecan with pembrolizumab, platinum and pemetrexed in four hundred fifty-four patients with a Her two mutation. Progression-free survival was fourteen point three versus eight point three months. Overall survival did not favor the experimental arm: twenty-nine point three versus thirty-three point one months. Pneumonitis or interstitial lung disease occurred in twenty point eight versus two point three percent. First-line choice should be discussed case by case.

Artemis zero zero eight, an open-label trial with four hundred sixty-one patients, tested the conjugate risvutatug rezetecan against topotecan. The setting was relapsed small cell lung cancer. Median overall survival was eighteen point five versus ten point three months, with a hazard ratio of zero point four six. Critical reading: it is open-label and China-only; a global trial is needed before changing practice.

In hormone receptor positive breast cancer with an estrogen receptor gene mutation, the U.S. Food and Drug Administration granted accelerated approval to camizestrant. In Serena six, with three hundred fifteen patients, progression-free survival was sixteen point zero versus nine point two months. The agency also approved imlunestrant with abemaciclib. According to the Brazilian press, Brazil's health regulator approved camizestrant. By contrast, Serena four, in unselected first-line disease, did not reach significance. Liquid biopsy-guided switching has not yet shown an overall survival benefit.

In radiotherapy, a randomized American trial with one hundred fifty-one patients treated brain metastases from small cell lung cancer. It compared radiosurgery with hippocampal-avoidance whole-brain radiotherapy. It missed its primary neurocognitive endpoint. But radiosurgery was associated with longer overall survival: seventeen point four versus eight point six months. Survival was a secondary endpoint and the sample is small.

A Chinese trial with eight hundred ten high-risk patients compared fifteen fractions over three weeks with twenty-five fractions over five weeks after mastectomy. At ten years, locoregional recurrence was twelve point zero versus ten point three percent, with no significant difference. It adds long follow-up for hypofractionation, although it comes from a single country.

The Lancet published Roam, a randomized trial with one hundred fifty-seven patients with resected atypical meningioma. Five-year recurrence was fourteen percent with radiotherapy versus thirty percent with observation. It supports adjuvant radiotherapy, although the source reports no overall survival data.

In triple-class exposed myeloma, Cervino compared the bispecific etentamig with investigator's choice in three hundred ninety-three patients. The hazard ratio for progression was zero point four zero. The comparators are weak and the data come from AbbVie, with no peer-reviewed publication yet.

An advisory panel of the U.S. agency backed the Galleri multi-cancer test. Twelve-month sensitivity was thirty-five point zero and thirty-one point six percent in two studies. The vote is not an approval, and the test should not displace breast, cervical and colon screening.

Finally, the U.S. agency approved belzutifan with lenvatinib for clear cell renal cell carcinoma after immunotherapy. Against cabozantinib, progression-free survival was fourteen point six versus ten point six months. Overall survival showed no significant difference. It is a second-line option with high toxicity.

Always verify the primary source before applying any data. Thank you for listening. Until next month.
