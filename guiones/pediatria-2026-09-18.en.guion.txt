Good morning, colleagues. This bulletin covers August nineteenth to September eighteenth, twenty twenty-six.

The Champs surveillance network published eight years of data on causes of death in seven countries in Africa and South Asia. Eight thousand five hundred deaths of stillbirths, neonates and children under five had a complete investigation. Neonatal deaths were dominated by prematurity, asphyxia and sepsis, mostly gram-negative. Eighty point seven percent of assessed deaths were judged preventable or possibly preventable. Critical reading: the catchment areas are not nationally representative, and preventability is an expert judgment. Still, the burden of asphyxia and gram-negative sepsis transfers directly to Dominican units.

The Pan American Health Organization counts fifty-two thousand six hundred seventy-six confirmed measles cases in the Americas through September fifth. That is three point eight times the same period of twenty twenty-five, with sixty-one deaths. The recent trend is downward. Critical reading: the magnitude calls for strict surveillance of fever with rash and verification of two doses of measles, mumps and rubella vaccine. The risk of importation through tourism and migration is high.

The American Academy of Pediatrics expands respiratory syncytial virus immunization in the second season, for ages eight to nineteen months. It adds prematurity under thirty-two weeks, significant congenital heart disease, neuromuscular disorders and Down syndrome. Nirsevimab is the only product indicated in the second season. Critical reading: the expansion relies more on epidemiological risk than on subgroup trials. In the Dominican Republic, it guides private prescribing.

The United States Food and Drug Administration approved apitegromab, the first muscle-directed drug for spinal muscular atrophy. It is indicated from age two in patients already on background therapy. In the Sapphire trial, the motor function scale improved by two point two points versus placebo. An improvement of three points or more occurred in thirty-four point two versus thirteen point five percent. Critical reading: it is an add-on treatment with a modest effect. Fracture risk and cost are the practical limitations.

The agency also approved Fayuvi, the first gene therapy for Sanfilippo syndrome type A. It is a single intravenous dose. Approval was based on a single-arm study in children aged two to five, compared with a historical cohort. Treated children maintained or improved cognitive function. Critical reading: it is the first option in a lethal neurodegenerative disease, but the announcement gives no effect figures. Long-term safety follow-up is mandatory.

Novo Nordisk announced results of the Step Young trial of weekly semaglutide in one hundred sixty-five children aged six to eleven with obesity. At sixty-eight weeks, forty point four percent of treated children left the obesity range, versus zero percent with placebo. Critical reading: this is a preliminary announcement without peer review or the magnitude of the primary endpoint. Long-term safety on growth and puberty remains unresolved.

In hypochondroplasia, a phase three vosoritide trial was published in New England Journal of Medicine Evidence. According to BioMarin, growth velocity was two point three three centimeters per year greater than with placebo. Critical reading: it is a one-year, manufacturer-sponsored trial with a surrogate endpoint, not adult height.

In an adolescent subgroup of the Valiant trial, pegcetacoplan reduced proteinuria by seventy-five percent versus placebo at twenty-six weeks. It included fifty-five adolescents with complement three glomerulopathy or immune-complex membranoproliferative glomerulonephritis. Critical reading: the subgroup is underpowered, with nominal p values. Vaccination is required because of meningococcal infection risk.

The American Academy of Pediatrics recommends universal Covid vaccination from six to twenty-three months. From two to eighteen years, it recommends a single dose for children at increased risk of severe disease. Critical reading: it diverges from the recent federal schedule, and the effectiveness evidence is observational.

In the Dominican Republic, national surveillance data through week twenty-nine show dengue rising, with two hundred thirteen confirmed cases versus one hundred seventy-three. Leptospirosis rose from eighty-five to two hundred fifty-three cases. Infant mortality fell from one thousand ninety-three to nine hundred ninety-two. These data were published on August sixteenth, outside the period. Critical reading: this is administrative surveillance data, not a study. The upturn in pertussis and leptospirosis calls for stronger vaccination and clinical suspicion in the febrile child.

In pediatric surgery there were no new verifiable trials. The agency's Pediatric Advisory Committee reviewed scoliosis devices, the Contegra conduit and Epicel. Critical reading: this is surveillance documentation, with no new efficacy data.

Finally, the Saudi Neonatology Society held its twenty-fourth international congress in Riyadh, with no verifiable clinical data in the source. Looking ahead, the American Academy of Pediatrics National Conference takes place in San Diego, October second to sixth.
