Good morning, colleagues. This newscast covers August thirtieth to September twenty-ninth, two thousand twenty-six. The month was dominated by uro-oncology, with little verifiable material in stone disease.

The British Pace A trial, presented at the American Society for Radiation Oncology meeting, randomized one hundred twenty-three men with localized prostate cancer. It compared stereotactic body radiotherapy with radical prostatectomy. At eight years, freedom from biochemical or clinical failure was ninety-one versus eighty-four percent, a difference that was not significant. At five years, forty-eight percent of surgical patients used at least one pad daily, versus eight percent of irradiated patients. Critical reading: these are secondary endpoints of a small trial not powered for oncologic equivalence. In the Dominican Republic, this radiotherapy is available at few centers.

The United States drug agency approved belzutifan plus lenvatinib for advanced clear cell renal cell carcinoma after immunotherapy. In the Litespark Eleven trial, versus cabozantinib, median progression-free survival was fourteen point six versus ten point six months. Overall survival was not significant. Critical reading: per Merck, serious adverse reactions occurred in fifty-four percent of patients on the combination. In our setting, cabozantinib will remain the reference.

In muscle-invasive bladder cancer, the European medicines committee recommended perioperative enfortumab vedotin plus pembrolizumab. Versus gemcitabine and cisplatin, two-year event-free survival was seventy-nine point four versus sixty-six point two percent. The United States drug agency also granted priority review to durvalumab with enfortumab vedotin. Critical reading: toxicity is high, and cost will keep neoadjuvant cisplatin as the standard in the Dominican Republic. The Volga trial has only press-release data from AstraZeneca.

An analysis of a trial of radiotherapy with or without short-term hormone therapy assessed the Decipher genomic test in three hundred ninety-five intermediate-risk patients. With an intermediate or high score, hormone therapy cut ten-year metastases by eight absolute points. With a low score, the difference was one point and not significant. Critical reading: it is a retrospective subset analysis publicized by the manufacturer, Veracyte.

The Radical P C Two trial randomized two thousand four hundred eighty-seven men starting androgen deprivation therapy. Referral to cardiology or internal medicine did not reduce cardiovascular death, myocardial infarction, stroke or heart failure. Critical reading: routine referral has not shown clinical benefit, and urologists can take on basic risk-factor control.

In Nature, a consortium sequenced nine hundred fifty-nine primary prostate cancers. Eight mutational signatures explain eighty-five percent of the tumors. One poor-prognosis signature was more frequent in patients of African ancestry. Critical reading: this is discovery science with no clinical test yet, but the finding is relevant to Dominican and Caribbean populations.

In Japan, of nine hundred eighty-nine men on active surveillance, only thirty-three point seven percent remained on it at five years. Critical reading: it is retrospective, but institutional culture matters as much as the tumor for surveillance persistence.

In the United States, magnetic resonance imaging before a first prostate biopsy rose from fourteen percent in twenty seventeen to sixty-four percent in the first half of twenty twenty-six. Critical reading: it is descriptive, and in the Dominican Republic access to quality imaging remains the bottleneck.

FemPulse obtained the European conformity mark for a vaginal neuromodulation ring for overactive bladder. The company cites data from four unspecified studies. Critical reading: no comparative data have been published, and the mark does not equal proven efficacy.

The United States drug agency granted regenerative medicine advanced therapy designation to a gene therapy for neurogenic detrusor overactivity. According to the company, it reduced incontinence episodes by more than eighty-eight percent in the low-dose cohort. Critical reading: these are preliminary, company-reported data without a patient count, far from practice.

At the European robotic urology meeting in Padua, eight live telesurgeries were scheduled. In a prostatectomy with the patient in Casablanca, the remote surgeon was slow to reconnect, and the Moroccan surgeon performed more of the case than planned. The connection was restored within minutes. Critical reading: telesurgery requires a local team able to continue the operation and redundant networks.

A phase one trial in sixty-two healthy men found that topical sildenafil cream has a relative bioavailability of two point six to three point two percent. Critical reading: it is small, funded by Strategic Science and Technologies, and provides no efficacy data in erectile dysfunction.

The German urology congress in Düsseldorf drew about six thousand eight hundred people, focused on artificial intelligence and sustainability. Critical reading: it is a barometer of the specialty in Europe, without definitive results.

Finally, the United States drug agency cleared the SpaceOAR Flex rectal spacer and the LigaSure instrument on the Hugo robotic platform. Critical reading: these are incremental improvements without new clinical data, although alternative platforms could lower the cost of robotic surgery in the region. Thank you for listening. Until next month.
