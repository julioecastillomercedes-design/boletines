**MedBulletins** is a clinical update project for healthcare professionals, started in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic. It is curated by **Aura Celeste**, with the help of artificial intelligence.

## What we publish

One bulletin per specialty (weekly for vascular surgery, monthly for the others), in Spanish and English, with full text and an audio newscast of about five minutes. Each issue covers what was published or presented during the stated period: clinical trials, meta-analyses, practice guidelines, regulatory approvals and recalls, and meeting news.

## How we select and verify

- We search journals, scientific societies, regulators (FDA, EMA and others) and meetings worldwide, including sources in languages other than English.
- Only items from the period covered are included. Exception: an item 31 to 35 days old may be included when its importance justifies it, and it is then labeled "Outside the period" with its date. Nothing older than 35 days is published.
- Every figure is checked against its source before inclusion; if the date or the number cannot be confirmed, the item is left out.
- For regulatory decisions we link, when available, the official communication from the regulator or the manufacturer, in addition to medical press coverage.
- Each item carries a critical reading: study design, sample size, funding and manufacturer conflicts of interest, and whether it changes practice.
- When we cannot access the full text and rely on an abstract or medical press coverage, we say so in the item itself.
- We would rather publish fewer items than pad a slow month.
- Before publication, every issue goes through an independent verification that checks each figure, date and population against its source; anything that cannot be confirmed is withdrawn or explicitly attributed.

## Editorial responsibility

MedBulletins is an independent editorial project based in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic, curated by Aura Celeste. Final responsibility for what is published lies with the project's editorial direction, led by a Dominican specialist physician. We are bringing in specialist reviewers for each area; as each one accepts, their name and specialty will be published on this page. Until then, accuracy rests on the method described above (a linked source for every item and independent verification before publication) and on this public corrections log.

## Sections

- **Bulletin:** what was published during the period, by specialty (weekly for vascular surgery, monthly for the others).
- **What's next:** between one bulletin and the next, what is coming in each specialty (trial results, regulatory decisions, guidelines) and what problem it aims to solve. Each item states whether its date is confirmed or estimated and links to an official or primary source.
- **Milestones in medicine:** specials on the most notable advances, such as the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine.

## Use of artificial intelligence

Searching, the first draft, the translation and the audio voice are prepared with the help of artificial intelligence tools, following the editorial criteria on this page. Artificial intelligence can make mistakes: that is why every item links to its source, and we ask readers to check the original before making clinical decisions.
