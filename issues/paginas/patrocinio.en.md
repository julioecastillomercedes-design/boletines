**Medical Bulletins** publishes, every month and by specialty, a verified digest of the most relevant scientific advances worldwide (papers, guidelines, clinical trials, regulatory decisions), with a critical reading of each study and a five-to-seven-minute audio newscast that is shared over WhatsApp. It is edited in Spanish and English and read by physicians in the Dominican Republic and other Spanish-speaking countries.

There are two ways to work with us.

## 1. Sponsoring an issue or a specialty

A company, laboratory, distributor, clinic or society can support the publication and appear as its sponsor.

- **Issue sponsorship**: your logo and a "Supported by …" line on the issue's web page, in the email to subscribers and a brief mention at the close of the audio newscast.
- **Specialty sponsorship (monthly or yearly)**: the above on every issue of one specialty, plus presence on that specialty's front page.
- **Portal sponsorship (yearly)**: presence on the main front page and on every specialty.

## Editorial rules that are not negotiable

- The sponsor **has no say** in what is selected or how it is written. The critical reading of every study, including studies funded by the sponsor itself, stays intact.
- Every sponsorship is **disclosed** visibly on each piece where it appears.
- No promotional content is published disguised as scientific news, and no sponsorship that requires omitting information is accepted.
- Drug advertising follows Dominican and international rules on promotion aimed at healthcare professionals.

## 2. A branded edition for your institution

Medical societies, residency programs, hospitals and clinics can commission an **edition under their own name**: the same worldwide search, verification and critical-reading method, applied to the specialty or subspecialty the institution chooses, with its identity on the page, the email and the audio, at the frequency it needs (monthly, fortnightly or weekly). Editions in English or other languages are also available.

## Audience figures

The portal shares its reader and audio-download figures with anyone who asks; they are updated monthly.
