Revised edition of the bulletin for the week of August 31 to September 7. A slow week for clinical evidence: ESC 2026 brought no peripheral vascular trials and CIRSE is still ongoing with no results yet. Regulatory and industry news dominate; 8 items, figures verified against the source.

## Priority areas

### 1) Medicare approves NTAP for the Spur recoverable stent (infrapopliteal) [Regulatory/Industry]
Up to US$2,596.75 per case starting October 1, 2026, for a maximum of two years. Basis: DEEPER REVEAL (130 patients, 49 centers, single arm). At 12 months, in vessels of 2.5 mm or more measured by a core lab: primary patency 78% and freedom from revascularization 83.1%; complete wound healing 84.3% in the paired analysis of patients with wounds. These are not figures for all 130 patients; the manufacturer does not report denominators. Why it matters: reimbursement, not new evidence; no comparator; manufacturer data.

### 4) Clopidogrel metabolism variants and cardiovascular events after peripheral intervention (VAM 2026) [Congress]
~20% carriers; no increase in limb events, but more major adverse cardiovascular events (including myocardial infarction and stroke) over the following year. Observational, no effect-size figures; does not support routine genotyping.
https://vascularspecialistonline.com/study-links-clopidogrel-genetic-variants-to-cardiovascular-risk-after-pad-intervention/
